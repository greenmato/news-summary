# July 2025

## Major Conflicts and Military Developments

### Russian-Ukrainian War
- Russia claimed control of entire Luhansk Oblast
- Key military developments:
  * Russia captured Pokrovsk and Kupiansk
  * Multiple large-scale Russian drone and missile attacks on Ukrainian cities
  * US temporarily suspended then resumed military aid
  * NATO took over coordination of Western military support
  * Trump issued 12-day ultimatum to Russia to end war
- Significant civilian casualties from Russian strikes:
  * 28 killed in Kyiv strikes
  * 25 killed including 16 prison inmates in Zaporizhzhia
  * Multiple attacks on infrastructure and residential areas

### Gaza War and Middle East Crisis
- Ongoing humanitarian crisis:
  * Over 58,000 total Palestinian deaths reported
  * Multiple incidents of Palestinians killed while seeking aid
  * UN reported 90,000 children and women needing urgent treatment
  * Israel began humanitarian air drops amid famine concerns
- Israeli operations:
  * First-time entry into Deir al-Balah
  * Strike on Holy Family Church killed 3
  * Multiple airstrikes across Gaza Strip
- International peace efforts:
  * New York Peace Conference launched
  * Multiple countries announced recognition of Palestine
  * 15-month transition plan proposed for Palestinian state

### Somali Civil War
- Al-Shabaab militants:
  * Captured strategic towns including Mahaas and Tardo Village
  * Conducted attacks near Mogadishu
- Constitutional crisis:
  * Clashes between Jubaland forces and federal government
  * Federal troops captured Beled Hawo
  * Puntland operations against ISIS

### Cambodia-Thailand Border Conflict
- Major escalation of hostilities:
  * 14 killed and 46 injured in Thailand
  * Artillery exchanges and airstrikes
  * Over 178,000 civilians displaced
  * Thailand declared martial law in border areas
  * Cambodian major general killed
  * Naval engagement in Gulf of Thailand
- Ceasefire negotiations began with US mediation

## International Relations

### US Trade Agreements
Trump administration announced multiple trade deals:
- Indonesia ($19.5B deal)
- Japan (reduced tariffs to 15%)
- European Union (15% reciprocal tariffs)
- South Korea (15% tariffs)
- Philippines (19% tariffs)
- New tariffs on various countries including Canada (35%)

### Recognition of Palestine
Multiple countries announced plans to recognize Palestine:
- France (September 2025)
- Canada (September 2025, conditional)
- Malta (September 2025)
- San Marino (by end of 2025)
- UK (if no ceasefire by September)

## Disasters and Accidents

### Major Natural Disasters
- 8.8 magnitude earthquake off Kamchatka Peninsula
- Flooding in:
  * Beijing and Hebei (70+ deaths)
  * Central Texas (104 deaths)
  * South Korea (17 deaths)
- Multiple transportation accidents:
  * Angara Airlines crash (48 deaths)
  * Baden-Württemberg train derailment
  * Wonder Sea boat capsizing (38+ deaths)

## Notable Political Developments

- Myanmar junta ended state of emergency
- El Salvador abolished presidential term limits
- Trump signed executive order restoring Presidential Fitness Test
- Thai PM Paetongtarn Shinawatra suspended by Constitutional Court
- Jennifer Geerlings-Simons became first female president of Suriname

## Law and Crime

- Bryan Kohberger sentenced to life for Idaho murders
- Sean Combs acquitted of sex trafficking
- ICC issued arrest warrants for Taliban leaders
- Multiple mass shootings in US including New York City and Reno
- Former Colombian president Uribe convicted of witness tampering
