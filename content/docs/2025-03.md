# March 2025

## Major Conflicts and Military Developments

### Gaza War Developments
- Israeli military launched new attacks and offensive operations:
  * Over 400 Palestinians killed in largest attack since January ceasefire
  * IDF re-took Netzarim Corridor and launched ground offensive on Rafah
  * Multiple Hamas leaders killed including Salah al-Bardawil and Ismail Barhoum
  * Accusations of war crimes with 14 aid workers found in mass grave
- Humanitarian crisis worsened:
  * UNICEF reported 1 in 3 children in North Gaza malnourished
  * Israel blocked humanitarian aid and cut power supply
  * Over 100,000 Israelis protested demanding hostage release deal

### Russian-Ukrainian War
- Major developments included:
  * US temporarily paused and then resumed military aid to Ukraine
  * Russia and Ukraine agreed to Black Sea ceasefire for commercial shipping
  * Ukrainian forces recaptured village of Nadiia in Luhansk Oblast
  * Multiple Russian drone and missile strikes on civilian areas
  * Russia claimed retaking villages in Kursk Oblast
  * North Korea sent 3,000 additional soldiers to support Russia

### Syrian Civil War
- Massacres of Alawite minority:
  * Over 1,600 civilians killed in sectarian violence
  * Majority of victims were Alawites and Christians
  * Mass graves discovered
  * Government forces accused of trying to hide scale of atrocities
- New transitional government formed under Ahmed al-Sharaa
- Syria and Kurdish forces signed agreement to integrate SDF into state institutions

## International Relations & Diplomacy

### US Foreign Policy Under Trump
- Tensions over Greenland:
  * Trump administration officials visited Greenland
  * Greenland PM called visits "highly aggressive"
  * New PM ruled out joining US
- Panama Canal controversy:
  * Trump ordered military to plan expanded presence
  * Administration considered reclaiming control
- Trade Relations:
  * Imposed 25% tariff on car imports
  * Trade tensions with Canada, Mexico, and China escalated
  * US suspended WTO contributions

### Other Diplomatic Developments
- Armenia and Azerbaijan agreed to peace treaty ending Nagorno-Karabakh conflict
- Germany reopened embassy in Damascus after 13 years
- Kenya recognized Kosovo as sovereign state
- Multiple countries including UK and Germany pledged aid to Syria

## Disasters & Accidents

### Natural Disasters
- Myanmar Earthquake:
  * 7.7 magnitude quake killed over 3,050 people
  * Significant damage in Myanmar and Thailand
  * 61 ancient religious buildings destroyed
  * National mourning declared
- South Korea Wildfires:
  * Worst natural fire disaster in nation's history
  * At least 26 killed
  * Ancient Gounsa temple destroyed
- Bolivia declared national emergency due to floods

## Politics & Elections

### Turkish Political Crisis
- Major protests against Erdoğan government:
  * Over 1,400 protesters detained
  * Demonstrations in 55 provinces
  * Opposition leader Ekrem İmamoğlu arrested
  * Largest protests in over a decade

### Other Political Developments
- Mark Carney became Canadian Prime Minister
- Marine Le Pen banned from running for office
- Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah became first female President of Namibia
- Alexander Lukashenko sworn in for 7th term as Belarus President

## Science & Technology

- Firefly Aerospace successfully landed on Moon
- German Spectrum rocket launch failed
- SpaceX conducted multiple missions
- Tesla recalled 46,000 Cybertrucks
- US nuclear-powered aircraft carrier program announced
