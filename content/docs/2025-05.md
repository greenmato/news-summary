# May 2025

## Major Conflicts and Military Developments

### Gaza War
- Ongoing heavy Israeli bombardment throughout the month
- Multiple days with 70+ Palestinians killed in airstrikes
- Controversial Trump proposal for forcible displacement of Gazans
- Death toll surpassed 45,000 Palestinians
- Hamas released American-Israeli hostage Edan Alexander after 19 months
- Israel announced control of 70% of Gaza Strip indefinitely
- IDF claimed killing of several Hamas leaders including Mohammed Sinwar

### Russian-Ukrainian War
- Russia launched largest drone/missile attacks since war began:
  * 298 Iranian drones and 69 missiles in single attack
  * Struck multiple cities including Kyiv
  * Major infrastructure damage and civilian casualties
- NATO allies lifted restrictions on Ukraine using long-range weapons inside Russia
- Largest prisoner exchange of war: 1,000 POWs from each side
- Multiple Russian strikes on Odesa killing civilians

### Syrian Civil War
- Sectarian violence continued with executions of Druze civilians
- Islamic State claimed responsibility for bombing in Suwayda
- US forces killed regional ISIS leader Abu Yusif
- Syrian transitional government facing ongoing challenges

## International Relations & Diplomacy

### Major Diplomatic Developments
- UK formally transferred Chagos Archipelago sovereignty to Mauritius
- US-China agreed to 90-day tariff reduction deal
- Trump announced 50% tariffs on EU goods
- India-UK signed major free trade agreement
- Argentina withdrew from WHO
- China announced visa-free travel for several South American countries

### Israel-Related Tensions
- UK suspended trade negotiations with Israel
- Multiple countries summoned Israeli ambassadors over diplomatic incident
- France threatened sanctions over humanitarian aid blockage
- Israel announced 22 new West Bank settlements

### Territorial Disputes
- Guyana-Venezuela tension over Essequibo referendum
- ICJ ruled Equatorial Guinea has sovereignty over disputed islands
- Cambodia-Thailand border shooting incident

## Domestic Politics

### US Political Developments
- Trump administration policies:
  * Ordered Harvard to stop enrolling international students (later blocked)
  * Announced tariff increases on multiple countries
  * Commuted Larry Hoover's prison sentence
  * Supreme Court allowed revocation of migrant parole program
- Elon Musk resigned from government role

### International Politics
- Australian Labor Party won federal election
- Nicușor Dan elected President of Romania
- South Korea held presidential election
- German AfD designated as right-wing extremist organization
- Nepal saw pro-monarchy protests

## Science & Technology

### Space Developments
- SpaceX Starship test flight failed to achieve goals
- Soviet Kosmos 482 re-entered atmosphere after 53 years

## Sports
