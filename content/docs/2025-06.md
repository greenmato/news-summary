# June 2025

## Middle Eastern Crisis

### Iran-Israel War
- Major conflict erupted with Israel launching preemptive strikes on Iran's nuclear facilities
- Key events included:
  * Israeli airstrikes destroyed nuclear facilities including Natanz and targeted military/government leadership
  * US conducted additional strikes on Iranian nuclear sites
  * Iran launched retaliatory missile strikes on Israel
  * Death toll reached over 430 in Iran and dozens in Israel
  * Multiple hospitals, civilian areas targeted on both sides
  * US-claimed ceasefire failed to take hold
  * Iran voted to close Strait of Hormuz in response

### Gaza War
- Ongoing humanitarian crisis and violence:
  * Multiple deadly incidents at aid distribution sites with hundreds killed
  * Israeli forces killed hundreds of Palestinians throughout month
  * Death toll since October 2023 reached 55,000 Palestinians
  * UNICEF reported 66 children died from malnutrition
  * Several hostage bodies recovered
  * EU found Israel may have breached human rights obligations

## Russian-Ukrainian War

### Major Combat Operations
- Russia launched largest drone/missile attacks since war began:
  * 472 drones and missiles in single attack
  * 500+ drones/missiles targeting infrastructure
  * Major strikes on Kyiv, Dnipro, Kharkiv killing dozens
- Ukraine claimed destruction of 40 Russian aircraft in drone attacks
- Ukraine lost first F-16 fighter jet in combat
- NATO and allies pledged €35 billion in new aid

## Domestic US Events

### Los Angeles Protests
- Anti-deportation protests led to:
  * National Guard deployment
  * Marine deployment to protect federal buildings
  * Legal battles over federal vs state control
  * Senator Padilla forcibly removed at press conference

### Immigration Policy
- Trump administration:
  * Banned entry from 12 "high-risk" countries
  * Suspended Harvard University visas
  * Increased deportations
  * Trade disputes with Canada/Mexico escalated

## International Relations

### Peace Agreements
- DR Congo and Rwanda signed provisional peace treaty
- Belarus pardoned opposition figures in US-brokered deal
- Finland withdrew from Ottawa Treaty (anti-personnel mines ban)

## Disasters

### Major Accidents
- Air India Flight 171 crashed killing 279 people
- Multiple fatal bus crashes and transportation accidents
- Severe flooding in South Africa killed over 100
- Natural disasters affected multiple regions globally

## Law & Crime

### Mass Violence
- Austria school shooting killed 11
- Minnesota legislators targeted in shootings
- Multiple attacks reported globally
- Significant drug trafficking busts and cartel violence

## Sports

- Oklahoma City Thunder won first NBA championship
- Carlos Alcaraz won French Open
- Florida Panthers repeated as Stanley Cup champions
- Coco Gauff won French Open women's title
