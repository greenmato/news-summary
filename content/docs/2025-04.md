# April 2025

## Major Conflicts and Military Developments

### Gaza War & Regional Conflict
- Israeli military operations:
  * Established "Morag Corridor" buffer zone between Rafah and Khan Younis
  * Controlled over half of Gaza Strip territory
  * Continued airstrikes killed hundreds of Palestinians
  * Death toll reached 52,243 since war began
- Humanitarian crisis:
  * World Food Programme reported empty food stocks
  * UN warned of widespread starvation
  * Over 332 children killed since resumption after ceasefire
- Regional developments:
  * Israel continued strikes in Lebanon despite ceasefire
  * Indonesia offered to accept 1,000 Palestinian evacuees
  * EU announced €1.6 billion aid package

### Russian-Ukrainian War
- Major developments:
  * Russia launched spring offensive with intensified attacks
  * Ukraine confirmed presence in Russia's Belgorod Oblast
  * North Korea confirmed deployment of soldiers to aid Russia
  * Russia declared three-day ceasefire for Victory Day (May 8-10)
- Notable attacks:
  * Russian missile strike killed 12 in Kyiv
  * Ukrainian F-16 shot down by S-400 missile
  * Russian Lt. Gen. Yaroslav Moskalik assassinated in car bombing

### India-Pakistan Crisis
- Triggered by terrorist attack in Pahalgam killing 28 people
- Escalating tensions:
  * India suspended Indus Water Treaty
  * Both countries closed borders and airspace
  * Pakistan warned of imminent Indian military strike
  * Military exchanges across Line of Control
  * Flooding along Jhelum River after water release

### Red Sea Crisis
- US-led coalition continued strikes on Houthi targets:
  * Strike on Ras Isa port killed 74 people
  * Attack on Saada prison killed 68
  * UK conducted airstrikes near Sanaa
  * US deployed second carrier strike group

## International Relations & Diplomacy

### Death of Pope Francis
- Pope Francis died on April 21 at age 88
- Funeral attended by 400,000 people including 130 world leaders
- Papal conclave scheduled for May 7

### US Foreign Policy
- Secretary of State Rubio announced:
  * Ready to abandon Ukraine peace efforts
  * 15% State Department staff cuts
  * Abolition of democracy and human rights office

### Cross-Strait Relations
- China conducted military exercises around Taiwan
- China seized disputed Sandy Cay Reef in Spratlys

## Economic Developments

### Trump Administration Tariffs
- Implementation of new tariffs:
  * 10% blanket tariff on most imports
  * 145% tariff on Chinese goods
  * 20% tariff on EU goods
  * Multiple countries announced retaliatory measures
- Economic impacts:
  * Global market declines
  * Supply chain disruptions
  * German economy forecast cut to zero

## Disasters & Accidents

### Natural Disasters
- 6.2 magnitude earthquake in Istanbul injured 359
- Wildfires in Israel forced evacuations
- Severe flooding in DR Congo killed dozens

### Major Accidents
- Port of Shahid Rajaee explosion in Iran killed 40
- Vancouver car attack killed 11
- Jet Set nightclub collapse in Dominican Republic killed 221

## Political Developments

### Elections & Leadership Changes
- Canadian federal election: Liberal Party under Mark Carney won
- Ecuadorian election: Daniel Noboa re-elected
- Klaus Schwab stepped down as WEF leader

## Science & Technology

- James Webb Space Telescope detected possible biosignature on K2-18b
- Colossal Biosciences bred genetically modified "dire wolf" pups
- First UK birth from transplanted womb announced
